05 / Reading the experiment

What this comparison means.

01 / THE ABLATION

One changed information channel.

The two workers share world, initial state, policy and seeds. The blind planner receives zero radiation costs; its real dose is still measured. Paths and subsequent observations can diverge.

02 / THE OBJECTIVE

Finish by exhausting frontiers.

A frontier is an observed support viewpoint with a view of unknown support. Visiting it does not remove unresolved unknown space. Blocked, tracking-limited and interrupted missions stay incomplete.

03 / THE SCOPE

Simulation evidence, not a guarantee.

Reference-floor observation is a fixed physical-arrival-gated 3D measure. Dose is a synthetic µSv-equivalent proxy. Compare matched progress and stop reasons; multiple seeds and layouts are needed for broader conclusions.
