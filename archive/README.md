# Historical material

[Project home](../README.md) · [Current status](../docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md) · [Forward plan](../docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md)

This directory preserves earlier research drafts, design plans and development
records without mixing them into the maintained `docs/` runbooks. Their claims,
checkpoint paths, links and proposed procedures reflect earlier working states;
they are not current instructions or evidence of completed work.

Use [the documentation index](../docs/INDEX.md) and
[the maintainer handoff](../docs/HANDOFF.md) for the current setup and next steps.
The archive was retained to avoid discarding the departing maintainer's research
context. No archived proposal should be presented as an implemented guarantee.

## Browse the archive

| Folder | Contents | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`experiments/`](experiments/) | Earlier navigation and integration investigations | Trace the motivation for a change, then check current source and retained datasets |
| [`research/`](research/) | Research drafts and proposals | Understand earlier hypotheses; consult the maintained literature review for the current synthesis |

For a chronological overview, use [project progression](../docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md#project-progression).
Historical reports retain their original claims and conditions. New evidence
belongs in a dated maintained report with a backlink to the relevant history,
rather than rewriting an old experiment as though it used today's configuration.
