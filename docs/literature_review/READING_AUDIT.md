## Meaning and limitations

Source response hashes, retrieval timestamps, authors, venue and available abstracts
are in `verified_sources.json`. The hash fingerprints the retrieved response; the
complete publisher responses and copyrighted PDFs are not bundled. An online refresh
can legitimately change a response hash as registry metadata evolves.

Supplemental abstracts are retained separately in `supplemental_reading.json` with
the matching title and arXiv link. They support content screening, not a second
independent replication. Full-text readings refer to the explicitly cited versions.

The paper-level numerical observations in the review are attributed to specific
tables in the four inspected competitors. They are not pooled or treated as directly
comparable success rates. All cross-paper interpretation and research-gap rankings
are the review’s synthesis, not statements attributed to any single source.

Before submission, extend the literature search, inspect all closest full texts,
check corrected/new versions, and independently review the novelty and claim audit.
